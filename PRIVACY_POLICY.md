# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Introduction

Takvimi për Shqiptarët ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

---

## Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the App may collect:

- Approximate or precise location (only with your permission)
- Prayer tracking statistics
- Quran reading progress
- Dhikr progress
- Favorites and bookmarks
- Nearby mosque preferences
- Nearby halal place preferences
- User profile settings (optional)

The App does **not** collect sensitive personal information unless explicitly provided by you.

---

## Location Data

Location permission is used only to:

- Calculate accurate prayer times
- Determine the Qibla direction
- Find nearby mosques
- Find nearby halal restaurants and services

Your location is never sold to third parties.

---

## Offline Data

Most application data is stored locally on your device using Room Database.

Examples include:

- Prayer history
- Quran bookmarks
- Reading progress
- Favorites
- Downloaded offline data

---

## Cloud Sync (Optional)

If you choose to enable cloud backup, the App may synchronize:

- Favorites
- Prayer statistics
- Reading progress
- Search history
- Collections

using your authenticated account.

Cloud synchronization is optional.

---

## Third-Party Services

The App may use:

- Firebase Authentication
- Firebase Cloud Firestore
- Firebase Cloud Messaging
- Firebase Analytics
- OpenStreetMap
- Overpass API
- Nominatim
- MapLibre

Each service has its own privacy policy.

---

## Analytics

Anonymous usage statistics may be collected to improve:

- Performance
- Stability
- Feature development

No personally identifiable information is shared for advertising purposes.

---

## Permissions

The App may request:

- Location
- Notifications
- Internet access

Permissions are requested only when needed.

---

## Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect your information.

However, no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

---

## Your Rights

You may:

- Delete your local data
- Disable cloud synchronization
- Revoke permissions at any time
- Request deletion of synced data

---

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

The latest version will always be available in this repository and within the application.
