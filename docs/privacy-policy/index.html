Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information that Takvimi për Shqiptarët may process, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Last updated: August 2026 · Application: Takvimi për Shqiptarët

1. Introduction

Takvimi për Shqiptarët (“the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information the App May Process

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the App may process:

  • Approximate or precise location
  • Prayer tracking statistics
  • Quran reading progress
  • Dhikr progress
  • Favorites and bookmarks
  • Nearby mosque and halal-place preferences
  • Optional user profile settings

3. Location Data

With your permission, location may be used to calculate prayer times, determine the Qibla direction, and find nearby mosques, halal restaurants, and services. The project’s published policy states that location is not sold to third parties.

4. Local Storage and Offline Data

Most application data is stored locally on your device using Room Database. Examples include prayer history, Quran bookmarks, reading progress, favorites, and downloaded offline data.

5. Optional Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to enable cloud backup, the App may synchronize favorites, prayer statistics, reading progress, search history, and collections using your authenticated account. Cloud synchronization is optional.

6. Third-Party Services

Depending on the features enabled in the App, services may include Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Analytics, OpenStreetMap, Overpass API, Nominatim, and MapLibre. These services have their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Analytics

The project’s published policy indicates that anonymous usage statistics may be collected to improve performance, stability, and feature development. It also states that personally identifiable information is not shared for advertising purposes.

8. Permissions

The App may request location, notifications, and internet access. Permissions are requested when needed for the corresponding functionality. You can manage or revoke permissions through your device settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The project’s published policy states that the App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on the data and features involved, you may delete local data, disable cloud synchronization, revoke permissions, or request deletion of synchronized data.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The latest published version will be available on this website and in the project repository.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the developer through the Support page or the project issue tracker.
